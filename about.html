Our Journey at ShipDeckKK

From humble beginnings to becoming a premier maritime dealership, discover who we are and what drives us.

Our Story

Founded in 2020, ShipDeckKK began with a vision to revolutionize the maritime industry. What started as a small vessel dealership in Marina Bay has grown into a comprehensive maritime solutions provider, serving clients worldwide.

Our headquarters, strategically located in the heart of Marina Bay, serves as the command center for our global operations. From here, we coordinate vessel sales, maintenance services, and logistics solutions for clients across the globe.

Today, we're proud to be at the forefront of maritime innovation, combining traditional expertise with cutting-edge technology to deliver exceptional service to our clients.
